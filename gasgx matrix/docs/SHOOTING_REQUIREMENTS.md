# GasGx Vibe-Matrix Shooting Requirements

This document is for daily field shooting. The current minimum workflow targets 30 generated videos per day with the least practical shooting load.

## Daily Minimum

- Total new clips: 30 to 40 clips per day.
- category_A: 15 clips minimum.
- category_B: 8 clips minimum.
- category_C: 6 clips minimum.
- Clip length: 3 to 8 seconds, ideally around 5 seconds.
- Format: vertical 9:16.
- Resolution: 1080x1920 minimum, 4K vertical preferred.
- Frame rate: 60fps preferred.
- Audio: not important, final output uses uploaded BGM.
- One clip should capture one clear object, action, or visual point.

## Upload Folders

Upload files directly into these folders:

- `D:\code\Chrome_Extensions\gasgx matrix\data\incoming\category_A`
- `D:\code\Chrome_Extensions\gasgx matrix\data\incoming\category_B`
- `D:\code\Chrome_Extensions\gasgx matrix\data\incoming\category_C`

The generator can read the newest files from each folder, so the crew only needs to upload today's new shots into the right category.

## category_A: Industrial Impact

Purpose: visual pressure and first-second attention.

Shoot:

- Miner fans, miner rows, miner LEDs, close passes over running equipment.
- Generator startup, running vibration, exhaust, gauges, mechanical movement.
- Sensors, probes, pipeline installation, wire harnesses, field sampling.
- Flow line: gas input, power generation, power distribution, miner operation.
- Test action: switching, plugging, startup, alarm state, pressure or load test.

Daily target: 15 to 20 clips.

## category_B: Technical Proof

Purpose: professionalism, ROI logic, and data credibility.

Shoot:

- HMI screens, monitor walls, dashboards, hashrate, power, temperature, alarms.
- ROI calculation pages, charts, control software, remote management.
- Control cabinet, breakers, PLC, wiring, electrical inspection.
- Theory animations: gas to power, power to hashrate, onsite monetization.
- Engineer hands operating computer, checking parameters, reviewing data.

Daily target: 8 to 12 clips.

## category_C: Brand and Close

Purpose: final trust signal and brand memory.

Shoot:

- Container exterior, open door, inside module, lifting, transport, shipment.
- GasGx polo, hat, glasses, team working scene, customer visit.
- Factory overview only after checking that old brand marks are not visible.
- Site case: installed equipment, running field scene, customer environment.
- Clean logo and brand details.

Daily target: 6 to 10 clips.

## Naming Rules

Use simple English keywords so the generator can classify and search clips later.

Examples:

```text
machine_miner_fan_001.mp4
generator_start_001.mp4
sensor_pipeline_install_001.mp4
flowline_gas_to_power_001.mp4
screen_hmi_dashboard_001.mp4
software_roi_calculation_001.mp4
control_cabinet_plc_001.mp4
container_open_door_001.mp4
brand_polo_team_001.mp4
factory_overview_clean_001.mp4
case_site_running_001.mp4
theory_animation_gas_to_hash_001.mp4
```

Avoid phone default names such as `IMG_1234.mp4` when possible.

## Quality Rules

- Keep camera movement steady and intentional.
- Avoid overexposure on sparks and screens.
- Avoid fully dark clips unless the equipment or screen is clearly visible.
- Avoid old brand marks, old logos, unrelated company names, and private data.
- For screens, shoot close enough that data shape is visible, but avoid leaking sensitive numbers unless approved.
- For people, prioritize hands, shoulder, back view, and branded clothing details if face approval is uncertain.

## Hard Stop

Do not upload factory overview footage unless old brand signs are checked and excluded.
